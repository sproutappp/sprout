# Sprout's Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to your use of the Sprout mobile application
and explains how Sprout collects, uses, protects, and handles
information provided through the App.

Sprout recognizes that your privacy is very important and we take it
seriously. This Privacy Policy ("Privacy Policy") sets out how we
collect, use and disclose your data when you use our mobile application
called Sprout ("App").

## Data Privacy Notice

This Data Privacy Notice ("Notice") outlines Sprout's commitment to
safeguarding and protecting the privacy of its users. As a data
controller, Sprout recognizes the significance of maintaining the
confidentiality and security of personal information. This Notice
governs the collection, processing, and disclosure of user data obtained
through Sprout's website, mobile application, and offline sources.

By accessing and/or utilizing our website and app, you explicitly
consent to the practices described in this Notice.

## Data Protection

Sprout is dedicated to maintaining the utmost data protection in
accordance with applicable data protection laws and regulations.

Under no circumstances does Sprout share any personal information with
third parties or individuals, unless required by a court order or
subpoena. Prior to any disclosure in such exceptional cases, Sprout will
diligently seek your explicit consent.

## Information Collection and Purpose

To ensure the provision of our services and enhance user experience,
Sprout may collect and process certain data, including but not limited
to:

-   Name
-   Email address
-   Phone number
-   Street address
-   City
-   District
-   State
-   PIN code
-   Country

Such information is essential for the creation of your user profile on
the App.

With your consent, Sprout may request access to upload photos, videos,
and other file formats to enable profile customization, post creation,
and messaging functionalities on the App.

Additionally, we may employ tracking tools like Google Analytics to
gather data regarding your App and website usage.

Sprout may utilize your provided contact details to communicate
information about special promotions, new features, or products.

By permitting access to your contacts, you can connect and interact with
your existing contacts and friends within the App.

We may also send essential account-related SMS or emails to keep you
informed and updated.

If your consent is granted, Sprout may request access to your phone's
microphone for audio/video recording or participation in live App
meetings.

## Promotional Calls

By submitting our web form, you explicitly consent to receive
promotional calls from third-party platforms on the number you provided.

## Account and Personal Data Deletion

At any time, you retain the right to delete your personal data and close
your user account.

To exercise this right:

1.  Log into your account.
2.  Navigate to the profile section.
3.  Access the settings.
4.  Click **Delete Account**.
